# Habita Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 5 November 2026_

Habita is a task manager that runs entirely on your device. It has no account,
no server, no analytics and no advertising, and it contains no third-party SDKs.

## What Habita stores

Your tasks, their schedule, and your settings (theme, chosen calendar) are saved
in the app's own storage on your phone. They are never sent anywhere. Android's
standard device backup may include them, under your Google account's backup
settings.

## Calendar access

If you grant calendar permission, Habita:

- reads events in the calendars on your phone, only to draw them on the day
  timeline so you can see what a time slot would collide with;
- creates, moves and deletes events, only in the one calendar you pick, and only
  for tasks you schedule in Habita.

This happens on the device, through Android's calendar provider. Whatever
calendar account you use (for example Google) then syncs those events the way it
syncs any other event. Habita itself does not transmit them. You can decline or
revoke the permission at any time, and the app keeps working with a local-only
timeline.

## Network

Habita does not request Android's internet permission, so it cannot send data
over the network.

## Data sharing

Habita collects no personal data, so it shares none.
